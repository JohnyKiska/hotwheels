List of 2013 Hot Wheels

Sign In to Save Save Edit
  • History
  • Purge
  • Talk (3)

For 2013, the Basic Hot Wheels lineup has been completely revamped. There are now only 5 main Series: 50 HW City cars, 25 HW Imagination Cars, 25 HW Stunt cars, 50 HW Racing Cars, and 100 HW Showroom cars, for a total of 250 cars for the year.

There is no longer a specific segment for New Models, new castings are interspersed throughout the mainline.
