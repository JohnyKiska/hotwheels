This article is a list of main line Hot Wheels released in 2011.

For 2011 there will be 50 New Models, 10 series of 10 cars, 15 Treasure Hunts, 15 Track Stars, 42 Thrill Racers and 22 HW Video Game Heroes, totaling 244 cars.
