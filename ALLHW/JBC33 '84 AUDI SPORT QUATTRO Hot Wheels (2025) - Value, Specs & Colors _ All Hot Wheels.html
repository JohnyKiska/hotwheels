The story behind the '84 AUDI SPORT QUATTRO

The '84 Audi Sport Quattro brings serious rally heritage to your shelf in the Wild Widebody series for 2025. As a Treasure Hunt model, this release is much harder to find than standard Mainline cars and carries more weight with seasoned collectors. The casting captures the aggressive wide fenders and sharp angles of one of the most iconic Group B rally machines ever built. It features detailed Castrol racing graphics that pop against the metallic silver finish. With its low production numbers compared to regular releases, this car is a prime target for hunters scanning store racks. You will know you have found a genuine Treasure Hunt when you spot the small red flame logo on the packaging or base. This model perfectly balances nostalgic motorsport history with modern diecast appeal.

Materials, colors and value

This specific casting comes in at the standard Hot Wheels scale of 1/64 and is made from plastic for durability during play and display. The car sports a striking silver paint job that gives it a high-quality metallic look without using metal body panels. Black wheels with white rims complement the bold orange racing stripes across the doors and rear quarter panel. Clear see-through windows allow you to view the black interior details clearly. Because this is a limited Treasure Hunt edition, these models often hold their value better than common cars once they are removed from packs. Collectors prize the unique livery and scarcity.

Questions about the '84 AUDI SPORT QUATTRO

how do i know if my audi quattro is a treasure hunt+

Look for the distinct red flame logo on the packaging box or printed directly onto the base of the car. This symbol marks it as part of the limited Treasure Hunt lineup, which means fewer units were produced compared to regular Mainline releases found in most stores.

is the 2025 wild widebody audi quattro hard to find+

Yes, this model is significantly harder to locate than standard cars. Since it belongs to the Treasure Hunt tier, Hot Wheels produces fewer of them for distribution. You may need to check multiple stores or hunt online marketplaces after initial releases sell out quickly.

what are the colors on this audi sport quattro diecast+

The car features a shiny silver body with prominent orange and black racing decals that include Castrol branding. It sits on black wheels with white accents, while the windows remain clear to show off the black interior details inside the cabin.
