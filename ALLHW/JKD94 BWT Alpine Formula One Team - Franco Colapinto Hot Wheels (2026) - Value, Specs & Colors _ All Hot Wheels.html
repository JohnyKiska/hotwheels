The story behind the BWT Alpine Formula One Team - Franco Colapinto

The BWT Alpine Formula One Team, Franco Colapinto brings high-speed racing action right onto your shelf for the 2026 season. Part of the official Hot Wheels Formula 1 assortment, this model celebrates a major moment in motorsport history. It features driver Franco Colapinto behind the wheel of his race car during the early stages of the year. The design captures the bold new look adopted by the team, utilizing a striking combination of matte black and vibrant pink graphics. Collectors who enjoy following real-world racing teams will find this release particularly engaging. It is included in Formula One Collection Mix 4 for 2026, which groups together specific entries from that competitive season.

Materials, colors and value

This die-cast model features a metal base with detailed plastic components to replicate the aerodynamic body of an actual F1 car. The paint scheme follows the provided facts and image closely. It uses matte black as the foundation for much of the chassis, while bright pink highlights define key areas like the front wing endplates and nose section. Blue accents appear on the sidepods and rear wing to complete the authentic team livery. All interior details are finished in solid black plastic. As a 1/64 scale vehicle designed by Rob Matthes and Manson Cheung, it offers excellent detail for its size.

Questions about the BWT Alpine Formula One Team - Franco Colapinto

What is the value of this Franco Colapinto Hot Wheels car?+

Since this model belongs to the standard Formula 1 assortment rather than a limited series like Treasure Hunts or Super Treasure Hunts, it remains widely available. Standard retail models generally hold their initial price well but do not typically appreciate significantly over time unless they are sealed in rare multi-packs.

How can I identify the correct BWT Alpine Colapinto version?+

Check for SKU JKD94 printed on the card or packaging. Look specifically for the driver number 43 displayed prominently near the cockpit area. The color combination of matte black mixed with bright pink and blue graphics is unique to this specific release from Formula One Collection Mix 4.

Is this a rare collectible diecast car?+

No, it is not considered ultra-rare. It falls under the general Formula 1 category which aims to provide accessible models for fans of global racing teams. While highly desirable for enthusiasts completing an F1 lineup or those following driver Franco Colapinto, production numbers are usually higher than special variants.
