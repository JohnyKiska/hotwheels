The story behind the 2015 Toyota Alphard

The 2015 Toyota Alphard brings a touch of high-end luxury to your Hot Wheels collection in the 2026 release schedule. This model is part of the Boulevard assortment, which focuses on showcasing iconic street vehicles and custom rides with serious style. Designed by Ronald Wong, this diecast car captures the sleek profile of a popular Japanese minivan known for its presence on city streets worldwide. It belongs to the Hot Wheels Boulevard Mix 3 set released in 2026, making it a fresh addition for fans of modern automotive design. The vehicle features sharp lines and premium detailing that stand out when displayed.

This diecast model is built with a metal body construction, giving it a solid feel and lasting durability compared to standard plastic toys.
